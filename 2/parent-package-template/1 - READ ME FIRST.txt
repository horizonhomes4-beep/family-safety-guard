FAMILY SAFETY GUARD
Installation for your child's laptop
============================================================

This package was made ONLY for ONE child's laptop (the one the
person who sent it to you told you about).
Please do not install it on any other computer.

You do not need any computer knowledge. Just follow the steps.
It takes about 10 minutes.


BEFORE YOU START
------------------------------------------------------------
* Use the child's laptop and connect it to the internet.
* Be signed in to the laptop with the PARENT (owner) account,
  not the child's account. Windows may ask for this account's
  password during installation.
* Tip: the child's own Windows account should be a "Standard"
  account. If it is an "Administrator" account, a child can
  switch the protection off. To check:
  Settings > Accounts > Family & other users > click the child's
  account > Change account type > choose "Standard User".


STEP 1 - EXTRACT (UNZIP) THE FOLDER
------------------------------------------------------------
Right-click the ZIP file you received and choose "Extract All...",
then click "Extract".

IMPORTANT: Do not run anything from inside the ZIP window.
Always extract first. A new normal folder will appear.


STEP 2 - INSTALL
------------------------------------------------------------
Open the new folder. Double-click the file called:

      2 - DOUBLE-CLICK TO INSTALL

(It may show as "2 - DOUBLE-CLICK TO INSTALL.bat".)

* If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC":
  click "More info", then click "Run anyway".
* When Windows asks "Do you want to allow this app to make
  changes to your device?" click YES.
* A black window will open and show many lines of text.
  This is normal. Wait. It can take 5 to 10 minutes and may
  look frozen for a while. Do NOT close it and do NOT turn off
  the laptop.


STEP 3 - CHECK THE RESULT
------------------------------------------------------------
* GREEN window saying SUCCESS: it worked. Press any key.
* RED window: it did not work. Take a photo of the screen and
  send it to the person who sent you this package.


STEP 4 - TELL US
------------------------------------------------------------
Send a short message to the person who sent you this package:
"It is installed." They will confirm that the laptop is
connected.


STEP 5 - CLEAN UP (important)
------------------------------------------------------------
Delete the ZIP file and this whole folder, then empty the
Recycle Bin. This package contains a private code for this
laptop. Do not share it or keep it where others can find it.
You do not need it any more.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
------------------------------------------------------------
* Nothing happens, or a message says files are missing:
  you probably did not extract the ZIP first. Go back to Step 1.
* Your antivirus shows a warning: do not click around. Take a
  photo of the warning and send it to us before continuing.
* The black window shows red text: send us a photo of it.


TO REMOVE THE PROGRAM LATER
------------------------------------------------------------
Settings > Apps > Installed apps (or "Apps & features"),
find "@M Family Safety Guard", click Uninstall.
(Only an administrator account can do this.)
